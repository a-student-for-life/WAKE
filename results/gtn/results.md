# GeoTrackNet results (addendum 1)

Generated by `gtn/analyse_gtn.py`; tables in `results/gtn/tables/` (file named under each table). Method as in `ANALYSIS_PLAN_ADDENDUM_1.md` ("Scoring", "Tests and reading rules") with the AUC, permutation test and bootstrap of `stage_b/analyse.py`: pooled over incident-control pairs (ties half); permutation p two-sided, 10,000 permutations within matched sets; 95% bootstrap interval, 5,000 resamples of events; seed 20261022. Score `d7_gtn` = -log10(smallest NFA); higher = more anomalous. GeoTrackNet is one pre-specified test at alpha 0.05, outside the Holm family of detectors 1-5. No interpretation beyond the reading rules.
