Case 1 of 5
How to decide (1 minute read)
  1. Name. Does the AIS name match the report? Small differences (ROBERT T. vs ROBERT T) are fine.
  2. Type. Does the kind of vessel fit? A fishing boat in the report shouldn't be a cargo ship or a pleasure craft in AIS.
  3. Size. Is the length close? Reports use feet, AIS uses metres; the table converts for you. Within about 15% is fine.
  4. Place and time. Was the vessel near the reported spot around the report time? Look at the map and the distance row.
  5. Behaviour. Does the track fit what happened? A grounding should stop; a vessel at a platform should hold position.

Missing AIS near the report time is common on rivers and offshore, so it isn't a no by itself. If the facts point both ways, choose Unsure.
